About Isomux

Isomux is a meta-harness: it runs Claude Code, Codex, and other agents as coworkers in a shared office, for individuals, teams and companies who want agents that work around the clock and are reachable from any device.

What Isomux does

A shared office for your agents

Each agent sits at a desk in a room, with a name, a look, custom instructions, and memories built over time. You see who's working, waiting, or idle at a glance.

Agents that work together

Agents find each other, read each other's current or past chats, and message each other. They track work on a shared board and schedule reminders for themselves or others, so work moves between agents without you passing it along.

Multiplayer, on every device

Invite people into your office with a link and pick which rooms each one sees. Everyone sees the same agents and conversations, updated instantly on laptop and phone, and anyone in a room can chime in.

A desk that comes stocked

Every agent has a built-in terminal, editor, diff viewer, diagram viewer, and URL screenshotter, and can use your browser. Agents can also build apps that the office keeps running for you and the people you invite.

Scheduled work

Schedule recurring agent runs daily, weekly, or every N minutes. Each run is a fresh session with a saved transcript, and you can resume any run as a conversation.

Hosted Isomux

For people who would rather not run a server: we set up a private server for your office at yourname.isomux.app. Agents run on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan.

What makes Isomux different

Agents are coworkers, not sessions

An Isomux agent keeps its name, desk, look, instructions, and memory when you switch it between Claude, Codex, and OpenCode. Each room has 8 desks, and each agent shows its state at its desk: sleeping when idle, typing when working, waving when it needs you.

Agents can run the office

Privileged agents can spawn agents, create rooms, set prompts, and manage schedules. Memory is shared at four levels - office, room, agent, and person - so what one agent learns can inform the others.

One always-on office per team

An office is one persistent server, where agents, files, and apps stay between sessions. Run it on your laptop, your own hardware, a VPS, Render, AWS, or Kubernetes, or let us host it.

Made for phones and for people who don't code

Install the office on your phone's home screen, speak your prompt, and hear the reply. New members join with a link, with no account or password, and the office reads in English, Spanish, Catalan, or Chinese.

Ways to use Isomux

Self-hosted

Run Isomux on your own machine or server. It is free for personal use, for organizations of up to 10 people, and for any non-production use.

Hosted Isomux

A hosting service we sell directly to customers. We set up a private server for your office, and you pay monthly for the server.

Enterprise agreements

Organizations of more than 10 people that run Isomux in production on their own infrastructure need a commercial license from Isomux LLC. Engineers set up the agents, non-technical employees build their own tools with them, and engineers join the same conversation when they need help. We are running a pilot with a startup.

The team behind Isomux

Isomux is built by Nil Mamano, its founder. Isomux LLC is the company behind it. Nil has a PhD in computer science from UC Irvine, was a senior software engineer at Google, and co-wrote Beyond Cracking the Coding Interview.

Isomux started in March 2026 as a nicer way to manage agents. Nil had a mix of local and remote sessions: for the local ones he had to keep his laptop open, and for the remote ones he had to ssh all the time. He couldn't check in from his phone and he had no good way to collaborate with others. The fix was to run every agent on a server with a browser UI, so all his devices see the same agents. The name is short for Isometric Multiplexer.

How Isomux works

  • Get it: clone the repo and run it locally, follow a hosting guide for an always-on server, or sign up for Hosted Isomux.

Frequently asked questions

What is a meta-harness?

Claude Code and Codex are harnesses around models. A meta-harness sits one level above them and runs them side by side. Isomux adds, among other things, messaging between agents, a shared task board, multiplayer, and a mobile UI.

Which agents does Isomux support?

Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode. Codex and OpenCode come bundled with Isomux; OpenCode needs Linux or macOS.

Do I need API keys?

No. Isomux works with your subscriptions: you sign in with your Claude or ChatGPT plan from inside the office. OpenCode also offers free models.

Is Isomux free?

Self-hosted Isomux is free for personal use and for organizations of up to 10 people, and anyone can evaluate, develop, and test with it. Larger organizations need a commercial license for production use. Hosted Isomux costs 8 EUR/month (Entry) or 17 EUR/month (Poweruser) for the server; agents run on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan.

Is Isomux open source?

It is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1. Each version converts to the Apache License 2.0 two years after its first release, and releases through v2026.9.10 remain available under the MIT License.

How is Isomux different from Omnigent and qm?

All three run several agent harnesses side by side. Omnigent is written in Python and licensed Apache 2.0; qm works in Slack and on the web and is licensed MIT. Isomux is written in TypeScript on Bun and puts agents at persistent desks in a shared office.

Where does my data live?

On the server that runs your office. A hosted office runs entirely on your own server, with no Isomux cloud service in between, and keeps seven daily backups.

Is Isomux ready for production?

It is in beta. Bug reports welcome.
