Isomux for Enterprise

Isomux comes as two products. Hosted Isomux is for individuals and small teams, on a private server we set up for them. Isomux for Enterprise is for whole companies: the company runs Isomux on its own infrastructure, under a commercial license.

Why

Isomux brings AI agents to non-technical employees, without the usual friction:

  • IT sets it up once; everyone else joins with an invite link.
  • A web UI on any device, nothing like a terminal.
  • Agents act like coworkers, which everyone is used to.
  • A built-in app store for the apps agents build: Isomux handles the hosting and access.
  • Fully multiplayer, so IT can step into the same agent chat when someone gets stuck.

A room of agents, with integrations and apps, can take over a business process and run it while the agents check each other's work. One process at a time, your company becomes AI native.

Options

Deployment. Run it on your own cloud or hardware. The office and its data stay on your servers, and Isomux LLC has no access to them. See the setup guides for common deployments like Kubernetes, and the sizing guide.

License. Isomux is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1. It is free for organizations of up to 10 people, and free to try and test at any size. An organization of more than 10 people that runs Isomux in production needs a commercial license from Isomux LLC. It includes 24/7 support, setup help, hours of Isomux engineering time each month, and priority for your feature requests.

Price and terms

Pricing. $25 per seat per month, or $20 paid yearly. Unlimited agents and devices per seat; model usage is on your own account. The first month is free, and setup help is included. For volume pricing or dedicated support, contact us.

Seats. You pay for one seat per member of each of your Isomux offices. Remove a member and invite someone else at any time, at no cost.

Support. An AI support agent answers email 24/7: within 15 minutes for a critical issue, and within 1 hour for everything else. An Isomux engineer works on a critical issue within 1 hour, 24/7, and answers everything else within 4 business hours (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm US Central time).

Engineering time. Up to 49 seats: 2 hours a month, and 10 hours of setup help. 50 seats or more: 4 hours a month, and 20 hours of setup help. Setup help is for the first 60 days. More time costs $250 an hour. Time on a bug or an outage caused by Isomux never counts against these hours.

How to start

  1. Try it. Evaluation is free at any company size. Run an office on your laptop (ask an agent to set it up) or follow a setup guide.
  2. Reach out. Write to llc@isomux.com, or to Nil on X and LinkedIn, or ask in Discord. As of September 2026, we are running a pilot with a YC-backed healthcare company.
