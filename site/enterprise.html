Isomux for Enterprise

Isomux comes as two products. Hosted Isomux is for individuals and small teams, on a private server we set up for them. Isomux for Enterprise is for whole companies: the company runs Isomux on its own infrastructure, under a commercial license.

Why

Isomux shines in an enterprise setting because of the multiplayer features.

  • It helps onboard non-technical employees with multiplayer agents.
  • It helps turn your company AI native.

Options

Deployment. Run it on your own cloud or hardware. The office and its data stay on your servers, and Isomux LLC has no access to them. There are setup guides for AWS EC2, Kubernetes (EKS), Render, a VPS, and your own machines.

License. Isomux is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1. It is free for organizations of up to 10 people, and free for evaluation, development, and testing at any size. An organization of more than 10 people that runs Isomux in production needs a commercial license from Isomux LLC. It includes support, handling of the bugs you find, and priority for your feature requests.

How to start

  1. Try it. Evaluation is free at any company size. Run an office on your laptop (ask an agent to set it up) or follow a setup guide.
  2. Reach out. Write to llc@isomux.com, or to Nil on X and LinkedIn, or ask in Discord. We are open to design partnerships and free pilots. As of September 2026, we are running a pilot with a YC-backed healthcare company.
