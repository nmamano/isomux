How it works

  1. Pick a name

    Your office lives at yourname.isomux.app, served over HTTPS.

  2. Pick a plan

    Monthly, easy to cancel anytime and export your data.

  3. We set up your server

    And give you a sign-in link when it's ready (usually <30 min). Nothing to install.

  4. Sign in and start working

    A Claude agent and a Codex agent will welcome you. You use your own Claude or ChatGPT plan to talk to them.

The same office and the same conversations from your laptop and your phone.

No update chores

When a new release is out, your office tells you, and applying it is one click.

What access we keep

Setting your server up is the only time we are inside it. When you tell us the office works (one click, or automatically after your first week), we delete the access key our setup used; after that, the machine doesn't accept our credentials. Only the people you invite can get in.

Disclaimer: since we rent the server, we technically have the ability to power it off, wipe it, and access the provider's rescue console, which can reach the disk. We never use any of it unless you ask us to in writing, and we log exactly what we do. The same is true of any hosted VPS service.

If you ever lose access to your own office (the sign-in link and every signed-in device), the way back in is one command on the server, which generates a fresh sign-in link. Add your own SSH key at signup and you can run it yourself. It also lets you sign in to the server to confirm that our key is gone. Without a key, recovery means asking us to run it through the provider's rescue console.

Questions

Can I run it myself?

Isomux is fully open source and self-hosting is encouraged (see here). Hosted is for people who would rather not set up their own VPS or home server.

Is there a free trial?

We may have some available - ask in Discord.

What happens if I cancel?

Your office keeps working to the end of the period you paid for, plus a week after that to take your work out. The office stays fully usable during that week; then the server is wiped.
