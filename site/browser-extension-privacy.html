Isomux Browser Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Isomux LLC ("we") publishes Isomux Browser, the Chrome extension that lets the agents of an Isomux office use your Chrome tabs. The extension works only with an Isomux office that you or your team run. We receive no data from it.

What the extension reads

  • The office address and the browser credential that the office sends when you pair. Chrome keeps them on your computer.
  • The content of the tabs that you offer to agents or let agents open, and of the popups that these tabs open, while agents control them: the page, what agents type and click there, and screenshots.

Where it goes

Only to the office that you paired with: over an encrypted connection, or a local one when the office runs on the same computer. The people who run that office decide how long it keeps what agents read. We, Google and other third parties receive nothing from the extension. It has no analytics and no ads.

What it does not do

  • It does not read your other tabs.
  • It does not collect your browsing history. Chrome warns "Read your browsing history" for the webNavigation permission. The extension uses this permission only to find which tab opened a popup.

Permissions

Permission Why
debugger Lets agents control a tab. The extension attaches when you offer the tab or an agent opens it, and detaches when control ends.
webNavigation Finds the popups that a controlled tab opens.
activeTab Lets the popup offer the tab that you have open.
storage Keeps the office address, the credential and your settings.
alarms Reconnects to the office after Chrome suspends the extension.

Stop and remove

Turn off Use this tab in the extension popup to end agent control of a tab. Unpair in the popup, or in Integrations → Browser Use in your office, revokes the browser. When you remove the extension, Chrome deletes what it stored.

Hosted Isomux

If your office is a Hosted Isomux office, the Hosted Isomux privacy policy covers the server that runs it.

Changes

We update this page when the extension changes what it reads or where it sends it. Write to llc@isomux.com with questions.
