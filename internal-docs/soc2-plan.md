## 1. Decision, scope and customer value

**Recommendation: start readiness now; do not buy an audit platform yet.** The published product serves personal offices and small teams at EUR 8 or 17/month (`site/hosted.html`). The design records zero paid offices on 2026-08-13, not today (`internal-docs/control-plane-design.md`). Current customer count, revenue and enterprise requests are unverified. With that evidence, an annual five-figure compliance program has no demonstrated return. Nil should buy an audit when a credible buyer confirms the required report, scope and deadline, and the expected gross profit supports the recurring cost. This is a commercial judgment, not a claim that small companies cannot obtain SOC 2.

SOC 2 is an independent CPA examination and report on a defined service and its controls, not a product certification. A Type 1 addresses the system description and control design at a date; Type 2 also tests operation over a period. Customers use that evidence in supplier risk review. It can remove a purchasing condition; it does not assure safe model outputs, replace all questionnaires, or guarantee a sale. Sources: [AICPA SOC overview](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services), [Schellman report types](https://www.schellman.com/services/soc-compliance-and-attestations). Recommendation: confirm buyer acceptance of the CPA firm and a short first reporting period before paying; Type 1 is optional, useful only if a buyer accepts it while Type 2 follows.

**Proposed boundary:** Hosted Isomux provisioning, billing, dashboard, DNS/TLS, support/rescue, development and release operations; customer VPS configuration and the duties retained after handoff. Include Nil's administrative devices, identities and agents that can change this service. Record customer duties and provider dependencies explicitly. Removing SSH access does not remove provider-account risk. Do not install compliance collectors that expose customer content or restore standing access. Sources: `internal-docs/control-plane-design.md`, `control-plane/README.md`, `site/hosted-terms.html`, `site/hosted-privacy.html`; scope proposal is this report's judgment. The older `internal-docs/hosted-isomux-design.md` contains superseded plans, not proof of current controls.

Security is mandatory. Availability and Confidentiality are common additions ([Sensiba scope guidance](https://sensiba.com/resources/insights/what-does-soc-2-cost/)). For this product, **Security + Confidentiality** is the proposed starting scope because offices hold code, conversations and credentials. Buyers that depend on continuous agent work may ask for **Availability**; the current terms promise local backups but no added uptime guarantee. **Processing Integrity** may matter for contracted automated business transactions; it should not imply model answers are correct. **Privacy** may be requested for personal-data workloads; collecting account data alone does not require adding that category. Privacy duties still need controls. These demand predictions are unverified; Nil must ask buyers. Category definitions: [AICPA Trust Services Criteria](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022). Product commitments: the three hosted HTML files above.

Type 2 periods commonly span 3–12 months ([Schellman](https://www.schellman.com/blog/crypto-and-blockchain/soc-benefits-for-blockchain)); three months is not a universal statutory minimum. Agree the period with the auditor and buyer. **Planning estimates from signup:** 4–8 weeks of readiness plus 2–4 weeks of fieldwork/reporting gives Type 1 in roughly 6–12 weeks. Add an agreed 3–6-month operating period for a first Type 2: roughly 5–9 months total; a 12-month period means roughly 14–15 months. Gaps below can extend this. A platform signup does not start valid evidence history automatically. Plan annual follow-on reporting and continuing control work; no vendor-specific delivery date was verified.
