# Figure It Out

You asked an office member for input on a decision. To the member, it looks like you have more context and enough judgement to make the call on your own.

If you agree, make the call. Be extra judicious, and do the necessary due diligence first.

If you do not agree, tell the member why you think they should make the call themselves.
