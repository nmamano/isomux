# Inbox

The member who typed /inbox wants your answer as an ask in their Inbox, to read and answer later, away from the chat.

1. Do the work the message asks for.
2. Put your whole answer in one ask to that member (see the asks reference page). Set `member` to the name in brackets at the start of their message, without the device. When the message has no member name, for example from a schedule, leave `member` out, and the ask goes to your manager. This is the exception to the short-ask rule: the ask carries the full answer, up to the prompt limit on that page.
   - Lead with the conclusion or the decision you need, then the reasons.
   - When the member must choose, add options and mark your pick with `recommended`. When nothing needs choosing, give one option, "Got it".
   - Attach images, plots or screenshots that help, as `readFiles`.
   - If the answer does not fit, cut it until it fits. Do not open several asks.
3. In chat, write one line only: the answer is in their Inbox, with the ask id.
4. Their reply comes back as a normal message. Answer follow-ups in chat unless they type /inbox again.
