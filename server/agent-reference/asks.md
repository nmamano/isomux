# Asks

An ask is what you need from one member to get unblocked: a decision, an answer, or an action only they can take. It stays above the chat input for that member and in their Inbox until they answer it. An office owner can also answer it. Before you ask for an action, do it yourself or through another agent when a route or tool allows it. Keep the prompt to what the member must decide or do and your pick, in one or two short sentences. Open one instead of asking again in later messages: the member still sees it.

Open one with `POST /api/agents/:id/asks`, where `:id` is your own agent id. Send the prompt as a text/plain body, up to 4000 characters. It goes to your manager; to send it to another member who can reach your room, add `member=<username>` to the query. For several members, repeat `member=` (up to 20), or send `room=1` for everyone who shows your room; the first answer closes it. With a quoted heredoc, nothing needs escaping:

```
curl -s -X POST "<office>/api/agents/<your-id>/asks" -H "<authorization>" -H 'Content-Type: text/plain' --data-binary @- <<'EOF'
Pull a per-agent spend breakdown before we change models?
EOF
```

The card shows bold, italics, `code` and links. `:green[text]` draws text in green; the other names are orange, red and purple. The text cannot hold a link. To show a text change, use a ` ```diff ` block: a `-` line shows in red and a `+` line in green. Put each `-` line right before its `+` line to mark the changed words.

A JSON body (`Content-Type: application/json`) takes `text` (or `prompt`), `member` (a name or a list), `room: true`, `link`, `step`, `options`, `recommended`, `priority`, `commands`, `editFiles` and `readFiles`. Query parameters work only with a text/plain body.

For an Integrations step (`connections` page) or a change only a member can make (`action-asks` page), send `step`, not `link`: the ask closes itself when it is done. To open another office page, add `link=<page>` (or `link` in JSON): the card gets a button for it. The value is a path, not a URL: `/tasks`, `/automations`, `/apps`, `/skills`, `/integrations`, `/settings`, `/inbox/pages`, `/inbox/asks`, or an Integrations tab: `/integrations/providers` (Claude and Codex sign-in), `/integrations/mcp`, `/integrations/secrets`, `/integrations/variables`, `/integrations/voice`, `/integrations/browser` (Chrome pairing), `/integrations/github` (owners only), `/integrations/pager`, `/integrations/api-tokens`, `/integrations/messaging`. `/agents/<agentId>` opens another agent's chat, in a room the member can reach. `/inbox/asks/<askId>` opens one ask, with its answer when it is closed. The same paths on the office's web address work as plain links.

For a few choices, or an answer you recommend, add 1 to 10 options: `option=<text>` each in the query, or `options`. Each is at most 80 characters, and they must differ. A tap puts the option in the reply field, which the member can edit; a tap on the option the field holds sends it. `recommended=<option text>` marks your pick and starts the field with it. A lone option starts there too: give one, such as `Done`, for a step the member does and then reports.

`priority=<level>` sets the urgency, `P0` (most urgent) to `P4`.

An ask can carry at most 10 chat affordances: `commands` for the member to run (`ask-commands` page), `editFiles` to open in the editor and `readFiles` to show in the card. Paths resolve against your cwd. A read file is copied when you create or edit the ask, except a video or audio file, which the card plays from its path with no size limit: keep it there until the member answers. In the query, add one `command=`, `editFile=` or `readFile=` per item, percent-encoded (`%20` for a space; a `+` stays a plus). A command is easier in JSON: `{"text": "Sign in to the GitHub CLI?", "commands": ["gh auth login"]}`. A prompt line of only `![](<file name>)` shows that read file there.

The answer arrives as a normal message from the member, starting with `[Reply to your ask <id>: "<prompt>"]`. If the member dismisses it, the bracket says `Dismissed your ask` and the message is `Dismissed` or the reason the member typed. If the member files it as a task for later, the bracket says `Your ask <id> is now task <taskId>`: go on without an answer, do not ask again, and do not start the task until someone assigns it.

A member message that starts with `About your ask "<the start of your question>":` (or the same in the member's language) is a question about that open ask, not its answer. Answer it in the chat and keep the ask open; edit the ask if your answer changes it.

When the member asks for a simpler ask, you get `[Simplify your ask <id>: "<prompt>"]` (`app <name>'s ask` for an app's ask). Rewrite the prompt in place with `PATCH`, not a new ask: what the member must decide or do and your pick, in at most two sentences, without internal names or ids. Until that edit the ask has `simplifyRequestedAt`; after it, `originalPrompt` holds the first prompt. If the member withdraws the request first, you get `[Simplify request withdrawn for your ask <id>: "<prompt>"]`: leave the ask as it is.

`GET /api/agents/:id/asks/:askId` returns your ask, open or closed. `members` lists who it is for now; if empty, delete the ask or name members. Edit with `PATCH /api/agents/:id/asks/:askId`. A text/plain body replaces the prompt; an empty body, or JSON without `text` or `prompt`, keeps it. A field you send replaces that field, and one you leave out stays: `link=` removes the link, a single empty `option=` (or `options: []`) the options, and `commands`, `editFiles` and `readFiles` work the same way. New options without `recommended` have none; send `recommended` alone to change only it, and `recommended=` to remove it. `priority=` (or `priority: null`) removes the priority. When the question no longer applies (say the member answered in the chat), delete it with `DELETE /api/agents/:id/asks/:askId`. Secret and sudo requests (`connections` and `sudo-requests` pages) are asks too; delete one to withdraw it. An app whose message target you are can open asks in your name (`apps-server` page).

To read a closed ask and its answer, an agent's earlier asks, or what happened to an ask before it closed, fetch the `asks-history` page.

Safe example: `GET /api/agents/<your-id>/asks`.

## Route contract

| Method and route                                  | Request                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Success                      |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| `POST /api/agents/:id/asks`                       | text/plain prompt, query `member`, `room`, `link`, `option`, `recommended`, `priority`, `command`, `editFile`, `readFile`; or `{text\|prompt,member?,room?,link?,options?,recommended?,priority?,commands?,editFiles?,readFiles?}` | `201` ask                    |
| `GET /api/agents/:id/asks`                        | None                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Your open asks, oldest first |
| `GET /api/agents/:id/asks/:askId`                 | None                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The ask                      |
| `GET /api/agents/:id/asks/:askId/files/:filename` | None                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | One of the ask's `readFiles` |
| `PATCH /api/agents/:id/asks/:askId`               | text/plain prompt, query `link`, `option`, `recommended`, `priority`, `command`, `editFile`, `readFile`; or `{text?\|prompt?,link?,options?,recommended?,priority?,commands?,editFiles?,readFiles?}`                               | The ask                      |
| `DELETE /api/agents/:id/asks/:askId`              | None                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | `204`                        |

An ask is `{id, agentId, memberUserId, member, members, room?, prompt, createdAt, updatedAt, link?, step?, options?, recommended?, priority?, commands?, editFiles?, readFiles?, secret?, sudo?, app?, simplifyRequestedAt?, originalPrompt?}`. Errors: 400 for a blank, too long or doubled prompt (`text` and `prompt`), or an edit that sends a step ask's `step` or `link`; 403 for an `:id` other than your own, and `forbidden_path` for a credential store path; 404 for an unknown id, or a closed one on edit or delete; 409 with no `member` and no manager, and `secret_request` or `sudo_request` on an edit of one; 422 for a `member` who is unknown or cannot reach your room, `invalid_members` for a bad `member` list or `room`, `no_audience` when nobody shows your room, and `invalid_link`, `invalid_options`, `invalid_priority`, `invalid_command`, `invalid_edit_file`, `invalid_read_file` or `invalid_affordances` for a field that breaks the rules above; 429 over 500 open asks.
